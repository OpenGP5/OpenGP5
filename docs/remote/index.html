Control your pedal over Bluetooth

Switch presets and turn settings on a GP-5 running an OpenGP5 firmware with Bluetooth.

  1. Plug the pedal in and switch it on.
  2. If a computer or another phone is connected to it over Bluetooth, disconnect it there: the pedal takes one connection at a time.
  3. Tap Connect and pick OpenGP5.

Settings change live; Save writes a preset to the pedal. Help
