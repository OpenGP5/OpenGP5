## Dreamcatcher

With [Dreamcatcher](../../firmwares/dreamcatcher/) on the pedal, the Presets page shows its 8 preset slots and an
editor with every setting.

- **Edit live.** Turn a knob in the editor and you hear it on the pedal at once. Changes on the pedal show up in
  the editor too.
- **Name** a preset (shown on the pedal), **move** it left or right, **clear** it.
- **Export** a preset to a file to share it, and **Import** one.
- **Write to pedal** saves the slots. **Undo changes** goes back to what's on the pedal.

Live editing needs Dreamcatcher 1.2 or later; tempo sync (Free / Sync, BPM) needs 1.4.

## Valeton's firmware

With Valeton's firmware on the pedal, the page shows its 100 presets.

- **Browse** the presets, and edit any one: the order of its blocks, each block on or off, its model and every
  setting by name, and its volume. You hear changes live.
- **Rename** or **reset** a preset; **export** and **import** single presets.
- **Clean up the pedal** clears all 100 presets, all SnapTones or all user IRs at once, to start fresh.

The model names come from your own copy of Valeton's firmware, which Launchpad downloads from Valeton on request.
Nothing of Valeton's is included in Launchpad.
