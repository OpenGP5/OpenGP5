The built-in capture (default_capture.inc, shown as "NAM A2 demo")
===================================================================

Source: example_models/A2.nam in NeuralAmpModelerCore (https://github.com/sdatkinson/NeuralAmpModelerCore,
commit b5a68c3e, "Add example A2 model"): an A2 capture (A2-Full + A2-Lite), 48 kHz, calibrated
(input_level_dbu 14.76). Its A2-Lite part is converted with tools/a2r_export.py (synth calibration
signal, headroom 4). sha256 of A2.nam:
2d2d744516dc0197737a2c5001010429692d4cb20d72b08264781de626fcf4ca
